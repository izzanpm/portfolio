# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is a recruiter or hiring manager evaluating Mohammad Izzan Putra Maulana for Data Analyst or Data Scientist opportunities. They need to understand his strongest work, analytical reasoning, technical range, and contact options quickly.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Mohammad's selected data work and professional background so an evaluator can decide whether to review his CV, inspect his project evidence, or contact him. Success means the visitor can identify his two strongest projects, understand what he contributed, and reach a working contact or CV destination without friction.

## Positioning

The portfolio earns credibility through legible project reasoning and evidence: the question, method, model comparison, result, and limitation are more important than a long inventory of self-reported skills.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically scan the site on desktop or mobile during candidate review. They may compare it with a CV, inspect repositories or project screenshots, and contact Mohammad by email or LinkedIn. The experience is a single-page portfolio with English and Indonesian language options.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing stack: Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and Lucide React.
- Static hosting only; no backend, authentication, CMS, analytics, or contact-form service is required.
- The site must preserve the current project, education, experience, certificate, contact, and CV facts unless the user supplies corrections.
- The Google Drive CV URL is the canonical CV destination until a local PDF is supplied.
- Smartphone Price Prediction and Breast Cancer Prediction are the featured projects.
- Repository URLs, screenshots, exact metrics, and project limitations will be supplied later. The site must not fabricate them.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the full name Mohammad Izzan Putra Maulana and the existing `IZN` shorthand.
- Present the role as Data Analyst and Data Scientist.
- Preserve English and Indonesian language support.
- Keep the voice direct, technically credible, and free of inflated claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- Seven project summaries and qualitative results in `src/data/projects.ts`.
- Education, experience, skills, and certificate content in `src/data/`.
- Contact links and a Google Drive CV destination in the existing components.
- No project repositories, screenshots, datasets, notebooks, exact performance metrics, credential links, or portrait are currently present in the project. Future work must expose honest pending states rather than invent these assets or claims.

## Product Principles

- Lead with work, not self-description.
- Prove capability with project reasoning and evidence.
- Keep high-intent paths such as CV and Contact accurate and immediate.
- Make every primary interaction keyboard, touch, and screen-reader accessible.
- Prefer a smaller number of strong case studies over equal emphasis across every credential.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The full experience must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, WCAG AA text contrast, 44px mobile touch targets, reduced motion, semantic structure, and correct document language for the selected locale.
